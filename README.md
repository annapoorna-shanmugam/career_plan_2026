# Career 2026 — 6-Month Career Positioning & Staff Engineer Plan

This repository is the source of truth for my 6-month career development plan.

## Goals

- Understand my actual market level
- Build a strong Senior/Staff Engineer positioning
- Strengthen technical interview readiness
- Build evidence-backed leadership and architecture stories
- Start and optimize the external interview pipeline
- Evaluate opportunities and offers objectively

## Repository Structure

- `month-01-career-audit/` — Career inventory, projects, metrics, leadership evidence, gaps
- `month-02-technical/` — Distributed systems, system design, payments, Java/backend
- `month-03-staff/` — Staff expectations, architecture, influence, behavioral stories
- `month-04-market/` — Target companies, recruiters, referrals, applications, interview pipeline
- `month-05-interviews/` — Interview feedback, technical gaps, mocks, offer optimization
- `month-06-offers/` — Compensation, negotiation, offer comparison and final decision

## Principle

Capture facts first. Convert them into resume/interview narratives later.

Do not invent metrics or inflate ownership. Clearly distinguish:
- Personal contribution
- Team-level work
- Technical ownership
- Cross-team influence
- Measurable impact
